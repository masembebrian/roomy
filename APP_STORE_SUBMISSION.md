## Review Guidelines

### Apple App Store Review Guidelines
- Ensure privacy policy is compliant with GDPR
- No misleading content or screenshots
- Test all payment flows
- Verify all links work
- Ensure app doesn't crash

### Google Play Store Review Guidelines
- Privacy policy must mention data collection
- No hate speech or offensive content
- Payment system must be compliant
- Test on multiple Android versions
- Ensure battery/data optimization

## Timeline

- iOS App Store: 24-48 hours review time
- Google Play Store: 2-4 hours review time
- After approval, updates typically go live within 1-2 hours

## Contact Information

For app store support issues:
- Apple: developer.apple.com/contact
- Google: support.google.com/googleplay
